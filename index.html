🌋 Indonesian Seismic Response Spectra (SNI 1726:2019) & Progressive Collapse Dynamics (DoD UFC 4-023-03)

SNI 1726:2019 ASCE 7-16 Newmark-β DAF EigenLab Playground macOS App Store Style

🌋 1. Indonesian Seismic Response Spectra Engine (src/core/modal.rs)

  • Standard Design Spectrum Formulation: Implementation of design earthquake response spectral acceleration curve \(S_a(T)\) per SNI 1726:2019 Section 6.4 and ASCE 7-16.
  • Site Amplification Factor Automation: Exact evaluation of \(F_a\) (short-period 0.2s) and \(F_v\) (1.0s period) across all Soil Site Classes (SA, SB, SC, SD, SE) using exact linear interpolation.
  • Accelerations & Transition Periods: Computes \(S_{MS} = F_a S_s\), \(S_{M1} = F_v S_1\), \(S_{DS} = \frac{2}{3} S_{MS}\), \(S_{D1} = \frac{2}{3} S_{M1}\), and characteristic transition periods \(T_s = S_{D1}/S_{DS}\), \(T_0 = 0.2 T_s\), and \(T_L = 4.0\text{ s}\).
  • Automated SDC Classification: Real-time Seismic Design Category (SDC A through E) classification and building response probe at fundamental natural period \(T_n\).
  • Indonesian Regional Presets: Instant seismic parameter presets for major cities: DKI Jakarta (\(S_s=0.72\text{g}, S_1=0.33\text{g}\)), Padang (\(S_s=1.35\text{g}, S_1=0.60\text{g}\)), Bandung, Surabaya, Yogyakarta, and IKN Nusantara.

💥 2. Transient Dynamic Amplification Factor (DAF) Engine (src/core/dynamics.rs)

  • Newmark-β Numerical Integration: Unconditionally stable step-by-step integration scheme (\(\beta = 1/4, \gamma = 1/2\)) for SDOF oscillators under sudden member removal.
  • Progressive Collapse Evaluation: Simulation of post-damage dynamic force surge (sudden member removal) per DoD UFC 4-023-03 guidelines.
  • Critical DAF Limit Audit: Evaluation of dynamic amplification factor against the safety limit \(\text{DAF} \le 2.00\) considering structural damping variation \(\zeta\) (1%–10%) and removal duration \(t_r\).

🧪 3. EigenLab — Interactive Computational Playground

  • Multi-Module Tab Switcher: Consolidation of 5 interactive computational laboratories into a single memory-efficient tab container (ARI & Gate 1, SNI 2847 Concrete Column, 1D CSO Rebar, SNI 1726 Seismic Spectrum, and DAF Dynamics).
  • Zero-Blur Retina Scaling: Application of setupHiDpiCanvas with window.devicePixelRatio adaptation for crisp graphics on MacBook, 4K, and mobile screens.

🧱 10D BIM Dimensions, Concrete Column P-M & 1D Cutting Stock Optimizer

SNI 2847:2019 Best-Fit Decreasing IFC 4.3 OpenBIM CSG Boolean Mesh 6D Carbon LCA

🧱 1. SNI 2847 / ACI 318 Concrete Column P-M Interaction (src/core/concrete.rs)

  • Whitney Equivalent Stress Block: Continuous computation of \(\beta_1\) parameter and concrete compressive stress intensity \(0.85 f'_c\).
  • Design Capacity Envelope: Determination of cardinal points for Pure Compression (\(\phi P_{n,\max}\)), Balanced Point (\(\phi P_b, \phi M_b\)), and Pure Flexure (\(\phi M_0\)) with transition strength reduction factor \(\phi\).
  • Interactive LRFD Audit: Control over dimensions \(b \times h\), rebar ratio \(\rho\), concrete strength \(f'_c\), steel yield strength \(f_y\), and automated Demand/Capacity Ratio (DCR) evaluation.

✂️ 2. 1D Cutting Stock Optimizer & Bar Bending Schedule (src/core/cso.rs)

  • Best-Fit Decreasing (BFD) Heuristic: One-dimensional packing algorithm minimizing offcut waste from standard 12-meter commercial rebar lengths.
  • Kerf Compensation & Reusable Offcut Classification: Automated saw blade thickness reduction (saw kerf) and segregation of reusable offcuts (\(L \ge 1.0\text{ m}\)) from net scrap.
  • Bar Bending Schedule (BBS): Automatic generation of rebar bending schedules and material yield efficiency auditing (yield efficiency > 96%).

🏗️ 3. OpenBIM IFC 4.3 & CSG Boolean Engine (src/core/csg.rs, src/core/bim.rs)

  • Solid Extrusion Modeling: 3D solid representation of walls, beams, and columns with Boolean subtraction operations for door and window openings.
  • BVH Spatial Ray-Picking: Accelerated interactive 3D ray-tracing on graphic canvas for LOD 350 structural element selection.

⚡ Adjusted Redundancy Index (ARI), SVD Gate 1 Kinematic Guard & Rayon Parallel Engine

Sherman-Morrison Gate 1 SVD Guard Woodbury N-2 Euler Buckling Kg(P) Canonical M1

⚡ 1. Linear ARI with Sherman-Morrison \(O(N^2)\) Rank-1 Perturbation (src/core/ari.rs)

  • Fast Post-Damage Inverse: Exact formulation \((\mathbf{K}_{ff}^{(-e)})^{-1} = \mathbf{K}_{ff}^{-1} + \frac{k_e (\mathbf{K}_{ff}^{-1} \mathbf{a}_e)(\mathbf{a}_e^T \mathbf{K}_{ff}^{-1})}{1 - h_e}\) eliminating the need for full global stiffness matrix refactorization.
  • Spectral Leverage \(h_e\): Projection of element flexibility onto global structural compliance \(h_e = k_e \mathbf{a}_e^T \mathbf{K}_{ff}^{-1} \mathbf{a}_e \in [0.0, 1.0]\).

🛡️ 2. Gate 1: SVD Kinematic Mechanism Guard & Hard Stability Certificate

  • Definitive Kinematic Determination: Spectral determinacy boundary check \(m_e = 1.0 - h_e\). If \(m_e \le \varepsilon_m\) or a rank drop occurs, the system certifies immediate collapse (\(ARI_e = 0.0, CI_e = 1.0\)) with zero false-positives.
  • Woodbury Rank-2 Simultaneous Screening: Screening thousands of simultaneous dual-member failure combinations \(\{i, j\}\) via the determinant of a \(2\times 2\) capacitance matrix.

📊 3. Canonical Model M1 Verification

  • Exact Analytical Proof: Verification of exact rational closed-form solutions with 58 unit tests passing 100% without numerical tolerance compromise.
